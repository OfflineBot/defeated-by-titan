# Combat

Combat is the exam for your movement. A titan is not a damage sponge to whittle down -
it's a routing problem: get behind it, get fast, pass the neck at the right moment.

## Blades

Two blades, left and right mouse button, carried as a pair.

The swing is a visible forward arc. The steel crosses your field of view exactly on the
frames that can connect. (An earlier build swept the blade sideways out of frame - you'd
swing, see nothing, and the titan dropped. Scrapped.)

Blades dull with use. Sharpness is a resource; spares are part of your gear. A landed hit
triggers a short hit-stop so the impact registers, plus a hit marker on the HUD.

## The cortex

The one lethal target: at the back of the neck, reachable from behind only.

It is not painted on. No glowing weak-spot ring, no outline. The neck area reads as a
slightly darker band with a faint amber core - the rest is knowledge. Players who know
the source material will look there anyway; everyone else learns it once and owns it.

Speed gates the kill. Below a minimum pass speed (around 20 m/s) the blade only scratches.
The pass itself is a short window - the skill is arriving fast AND timing the swing
inside it.

Body hits never kill but they matter: they stagger, they wear the blade, and on the
warden they're the key (below).

## Hit zones

Arms and legs register separately from the torso. The rule that makes it interesting:

The warden covers its neck with a hand. The guard only opens after a frontal hit on its
arms - so the kill is two moves: strike the arm from the front, then swing around for
the neck pass while the guard is down.
