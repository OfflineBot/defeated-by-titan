# Titans

Eight kinds. Size classes: small ~8 m, medium ~20 m, large ~28 m, huge ~42 m. Each kind
must be recognizable at a glance - build, posture, skin tone - and each behaves
differently. The behaviors are the content of the combat game.

| Kind | Class | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Husk | medium | The baseline. Slow, frontal, simple. The one you learn on. |
| Errant | medium | Fast and crooked - leans, weaves, breaks your approach angle. |
| Scuttler | small | Quick, short lunge sprints. Hard to track, dies fast. |
| Weaver | small | Evasive, only commits to what it can see. Dead-behind never opens it; you play the flank. |
| Warden | large | The guard. Hand over the neck, opens only to frontal arm hits. |
| Lurker | large | The ambusher. Stands, waits, erupts at close range. Does not chase. |
| Chorus | medium | The pack type. Calm, symmetric, comes in pairs, splits and flanks. |
| Bellower | huge | The caller. Hears gas bursts and roars, pulling other titans in. Rare - an event, not a regular. |

Other rules:

- Titans perceive. Sight for most, hearing for the bellower (it reacts to gas noise).
  Crossing rooftops quietly without boosting is a real option.
- Titans are themselves hookable. Riding a rope anchored to a walking titan is a
  legitimate tool, not an exploit.
- When a titan dies, every rope attached to it releases.
- Titans move like living things - walk, turn, strike. A titan standing like a statue
  is a defect, not a style.
