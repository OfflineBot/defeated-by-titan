# Scope

## Non-goals

These are as binding as the goals.

- Not a shooter, not a soulslike, not a builder. Movement is the game; combat is the test
  of it.
- No meta progression before the movement bar is met. Trait trees, economy, lineages,
  raids, cosmetics all wait behind the blind test (see inspiration.md).
- No horror staging. The titans are unsettling through scale, silence and the dead city -
  not through gore or jump scares.
- No hand-holding UI. No painted weak spot, no guide path, no minimap crutches. The game
  rewards knowledge and a good eye.

## Open questions (owner's call, by playtest)

1. How dramatic the terrain gets. Direction is decided - real relief, no flat plate -
   but the concrete shape (terraced city, gorge, a mix) is judged by eye on builds.
2. Gas costs and gravity as feel values. Always tuned at the controller, never finalized
   on paper.
3. How bright the cortex amber may glow before it violates the no-painted-weak-spot rule.
4. What the city's tall structures are. THAT they exist is settled (rope movement needs
   them, measured); whether they're dressed-up hoist works, bell towers or granaries is
   taste.
