# Defeated by Titan

Design docs for the game. Gameplay only, no tech.

Files:

- concept.md - what the game is, setting, priorities
- inspiration.md - the source material and the benchmark, what we keep and what we change
- movement.md - the vector gear and how moving works
- combat.md - blades, the cortex, hit zones
- titans.md - the eight kinds and their behavior
- world.md - Ashgate, terrain, traversal
- structure.md - game loop, progression, HUD, controls
- scope.md - non-goals and open questions

Everything in here was settled through playtesting on the previous build. Where a value
is still a matter of taste it says so.
