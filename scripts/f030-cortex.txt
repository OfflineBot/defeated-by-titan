# ## Why the player FALLS instead of flying
#
# `gear.ron: blades.min_speed_m_s` is 8.0 and `game.ron: player.run_speed_m_s` is 6.0 — a
# running player cannot cut anything, by design ("damage comes out of speed"). The Vector Gear
# reaches 30 m/s and more, but hooking, reeling and boosting cannot be aimed from a script
# accurately enough to pass a 1.10 m sphere. Gravity can, and it is reproducible to the tick.
#
# ## 🔴 THE TWO NUMBERS BELOW ARE DERIVED FROM `game.ron: gravity_m_s2`. DO NOT RE-AIM THEM BY HAND.
#
# **The constant this pass is built on is the SPEED at the nape, not the height and not the wait.**
# 21 m/s is chosen against two numbers in `gear.ron` and nothing else: it clears
# `blades.min_speed_m_s` 8.0, and it clears `feel.strong_hit_m_s` 18.0 by 17 %, so the pass is a
# CUT and not a GRAZE with margin on the low side. It is also a speed a Vector Gear player
# actually holds. **Everything else follows from it and from gravity:**
#
#     v      = 21.0 m/s                                   the pass speed  — THE constant
#     g      = -game.ron: gravity_m_s2  = 32.0 m/s²
#     drop   = v² / (2 g)      = 441 / 64   = 6.891 m     (was 11.2 m at g = 20)
#     t_fall = v / g           = 21 / 32    = 0.656 s     (was 1.05 s)
#     warp y = cortex_height_m 8.90 - eye_height_m 1.60 + drop   = 14.19
#     wait   = t_fall - (blades.active_from_s 0.08 + active_to_s 0.22) / 2 = 0.656 - 0.15 = 0.506
#
# The last line aims the nape crossing at the MIDDLE of the blade's active window, which is where
# it has the most room on both sides — the window is 0.14 s = 8.4 ticks and the cortex sphere is
# 1.10 m = 3.2 ticks at this speed.
#
# **The control that says this is the right derivation and not a fresh guess: put g = 20 back into
# it and it prints the numbers that were shipped here for ten days.** `v/g - 0.15` = `1.05 - 0.15`
# = **0.900**, the exact `wait 0.90` this file carried, and `v²/2g` = **11.03 m** against the 11.2
# it carried. A re-aim that could not reproduce the old calibration would be a coincidence, not a
# derivation.
#
# **So when `gravity_m_s2` moves again, change nothing here by hand: put the new number into the
# five lines above and read off `warp y` and `wait`.**
#
# **The freeze, re-probed on this run (2026-08-29, `assert height > 9999` every third tick):** y
# is 8.423 on 128, and then **7.765 on 131, on 134 and on 137** — bit-identical, three samples
# spanning the whole seven-tick window — and 6.711 on 140. 7.765 → 6.711 is 1.054 m over ticks
# 138, 139 and 140, i.e. 0.35 m per tick, which is exactly 21 m/s and says the fall resumed on
# **138** and not before. The repeated number is the hit stop and not the instrument.
#
# ## Why x = 15.75 and z = 0.8
#
# The husk's body capsule has a radius of `width_fraction × height / 2` = 1.25 m and the player
# capsule 0.35 m, so anything closer than **1.60 m** to his axis is a collision and not a pass.
# 1.75 m leaves 0.15 m of air. The blade is `reach_m` = 2.00 m long and hangs from the hand at
# eye height, so its tip reaches x = 17.75 — a quarter of a metre **past** the centre of a cortex
# sphere of radius 0.55 m at x = 17.5, and `thickness_m` 0.20 widens the swath around it.
# Until 2026-08-20 `reach_m` and the body radius were **1.60 m and 1.60 m** and there was no
# margin at all in these numbers; there is 0.40 m of it now, and that is a fact about the RON and
# not about the cut. The cortex also sits `head_m / 2` = 0.55 m **behind** the head's axis
# (`src/titan/rig.rs`), and z = 0.8 gives up 0.25 m of that offset — the blade still crosses the
# sphere.
