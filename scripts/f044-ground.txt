# F-044 — NAHKAMPF AM BODEN: the swing a player on his feet can finally land.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f044-ground.txt --ticks 400
#   # and the exit code needs its OWN run, a pipeline's $? is the LAST command's:
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f044-ground.txt --ticks 400 >/dev/null 2>&1; echo $?
#
# ## What this run is read off
#
# `game.ron: player.run_speed_m_s` is 6.0 and `gear.ron: blades.min_speed_m_s` is 8.0, so until
# 2026-08-25 a player standing on the ground could **not touch a titan at any speed his legs
# can produce** — `blades::cut` refused the hit before it ever wrote a message. That is the hole
# the row was opened for: *"Grundlegender Bodenangriff fuer Situationen ohne Gas."*
#
# The two lines to grep for:
#
#   tick N: cut titan 1 <zone> at 0.0x m/s      <- under min_speed_m_s, and it landed anyway
#   tick N: titan 1 took 5.0 in the <zone> ...  <- gear.ron: damage.ground_damage, flat
#
# **5.0 and not a speed term.** The cheapest cut a FLYING player can book is
# `damage_per_m_s x min_speed_m_s` = 1.4 x 8.0 = 11.2, so the ground attack is worth under half
# of the worst thing the air can do — the row's *"niemals die effizientere Wahl"*, answered in
# `gear.ron` and not in a balance pass.
#
# ⚠️ The husk aggros the moment the player stands next to him (`aggro_radius_m` 45) and hits for
# 34 of the player's 100. This run is deliberately short: three seconds is one wind-up, so the
# assert below still measures a player who is standing rather than one who is down.
#
# ## The geometry
#
# The stand-off of `scripts/f030-cortex.txt`, at knee height instead of at the nape: the husk's
# body capsule has a radius of 1.25 m and the player's 0.35, so 1.75 m off his axis is 0.15 m of
# air. `reach_m` 2.00 carries the right blade from x = 15.75 to 17.75, across the axis at 17.5.
# `look 0 0` and no camera move anywhere in the file — `blades::cut::blade_segment` builds the
# blade out of `intent.look_dir()`, so at yaw 0 the right blade lies on +X and at yaw -90 it
# points at nothing (`docs/FINDINGS.md` FIND-112).
wait 1.5
spawn titan husk 17.5 0 0
look 0 0
key F3 0.05
warp 15.75 0.6 0.8
wait 1.00
assert health > 0
mark on-his-feet
slash right 0.40
wait 0.80
mark ground-attack
# A ground attack is never a kill: `blades::cut::ground_attack` refuses the cortex outright,
# and `combat::damage::zone_factor` books 0.0 for it on top. Both would have to fail for this
# line to fall.
assert titans == 1
wait 1.00
assert titans == 1
