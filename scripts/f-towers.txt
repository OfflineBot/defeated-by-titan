# f-towers — THE RIVER IS CROSSABLE BY ROPE, AND THE CROSSING NEVER TOUCHES THE WATER.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f-towers.txt --ticks 1200
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f-towers.txt --ticks 1200 >/dev/null 2>&1; echo $?
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --offscreen --script scripts/f-towers.txt --ticks 120 \
#       --screenshot docs/images/f003-towers.png      # 120: inside ACT 0's own window, see it
#
# The user, 2026-08-29: *„adde andere tuerme beim wasser (das wasser ist auch VIEL zu klein)"*.
# Both halves landed together on 2026-09-01, and the second decides the first: the channel went
# from **10 m to 25 m** of water, so the towers are designed against a river two and a half
# times the one they were asked for beside.
#
# PINS: **the tower pair at z = 65**, out of `maps.ron: maps.ashgate.blocks`, and the channel it
# stands in. Move any of these and this file is wrong, not the map:
#
#   east shaft   (-55, 16.70, 65)      (10, 32.60, 10)  -> x -60..-50, y 0.40..33.00
#   east decks   (-58.5, 11/22/34, 65) (17,  2.00, 10)  -> x -67..-50, tops y 12 / 23 / 35
#   west shaft   (-90, 16.70, 65)      (10, 32.60, 10)  -> x -95..-85
#   west decks   (-86.5, 11/22/34, 65) (17,  2.00, 10)  -> x -95..-78, tops y 12 / 23 / 35
#   quays        x -95..-85 and -60..-50, tops y +0.40
#   water        x -84.95..-60.05, surface **y = -0.60**   (`water.ron: volumes.ashgate`)
#
# PINS: **the two stands and the two aims of ACT 2 and ACT 5.** They are a mirrored pair about
# the channel's centre line (x = -72.5) — the same crossing east-to-west and west-to-east — and
# re-aiming either makes the pair meaningless. When a constant moves under this file, the
# heights and speeds are re-measured against the same stands; a stand is never moved to recover
# a number.
#
# ⚠️ **The yaw sign, because it cost two runs.** `shared::Intent::look_dir` is
# `(-sin yaw, sin pitch, -cos yaw)`, so **a POSITIVE yaw turns toward -X**, i.e. west.
# `scripts/f003-wall.txt` said the opposite in a comment for three days; aiming this file off
# that comment hooked the wrong bank of the river twice, and the swing looked plausible both
# times. `docs/FINDINGS.md` FIND-220 §4.
#
# ## ⭐ THE MEASUREMENT, AND ITS TWO CONTROLS
#
# `game.ron: vector.rope_force_model` is **`Drive`**, so a hooked player who presses nothing
# falls — the rope is a leash, not a joint, until it comes tight. That is what makes the
# controls the evidence rather than a formality. All three rows, same stand, same tick, one
# binary:
#
#   WITH the hook                            lowest point **14.572 m** at 39.459 m/s, and he
#                                            ends **at rest on the far tower's rung 1:
#                                            y 12.000, speed 0.000**
#   the same stand, NO hook                  he falls straight into the channel and ends
#                                            **floating: y -1.294, drifting at 0.255**
#   the hook, and the far tower's RUNG 1     he swings the identical arc and ends
#   DELETED — one line of `maps.ron`         **floating: y -1.306, drifting at 0.509**
#
# The third row is what makes the first mean anything. **A body at rest reads its surface
# exactly** — 12.000 on a deck whose top is 12.00, 23.000 on one at 23.00, 0.400 on the quay,
# all measured — while a floating one sits at -1.29 (`water.ron`: `-gravity_m_s2 *
# surface_band_m / buoyancy_m_s2` = 0.727 m under the surface; `FIND-216` measured -1.292) and
# **never stops drifting**. So: the rope carries him over the river, and **one 17 x 2 x 10 block
# is the difference between the far bank and a swim.**
#
# ## What it costs, and it is not free
#
# **Gas 15000 -> 15000: the crossing itself is the rope and costs nothing.** What costs is
# getting to the deck. The rungs are at 12 / 23 / 35 m and the roofscape is 11.5 m
# (`scale.ron: house_large`), so a body coming off the eaves meets rung 1 at his own height and
# has **two rungs of climb** before the river is his — 23 m of reel or two swings, both billed.
# And `bottom = H - d` (FIND-041) says rung 1 is not a crossing at all: the two facing decks are
# 11.0 m apart, so rung 3 bottoms out at 35 - 11 = **24 m** and rung 1 at 12 - 11 = **1 m**,
# which is one metre over a river you have to be exactly right about. **You climb, or you swim.**
#
# ## ⚠️ THE SWING IS DIAGONAL, AND THAT IS THE MECHANIC, NOT A CONVENIENCE
#
# The same lesson the wall's ACT 2 wrote down: a pendulum's far half ends over its own anchor's
# structure. Hooked **off-axis** — 20 m up the river from the pair — the swing plane runs
# diagonally, its far half passes the far tower in Z, and the apex stands over it. So the AoT
# move here is the same sideways one the wall rewards, and the river is the map's strictest
# teacher of it: the penalty is not a bruise but a swim, in water that holds no hook
# (`vector::hookable::SurfaceKind::Water`) and bills the way out at
# `water.ron: swim.gas_cost_factor` 2.0.

wait 1.5                # world, camera, meshes and the render pipelines come up

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 0 — THE PICTURE. 58 m up over the middle of the channel at z = 130, looking straight
# down it: the pairs at z = 65 and z = -20, each two shafts the width of their own quay with
# three sand-brown rungs corbelled out over the water, 25 m of river running between them, and
# the 120 m wall closing the frame.
#
# ⚠️ The vantage stands over the OPEN channel and not over the bank, and that is not taste:
# from inside the district the near geometry fills the frame (measured three times on
# 2026-09-01 — a 50 m stand at x = -18 photographed the side of a house).
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -72.5 58 130
look 0 -11
wait 0.3
mark VIEW the river and its towers
wait 0.5                # ⚠️ THE SCREENSHOT WINDOW, and it is not padding. `--screenshot` fires
                        # at a TICK, the mark lands at t=111 and ACT 1's `warp` executes on
                        # t=112 — a frame taken at 115 without this line photographs the east
                        # quay from three metres up. Measured 2026-09-01, twice.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 1 — THE FOOT OF THE LADDER. He starts on the east quay 13 m north of the tower — beside
# it and not inside it: the shaft is the quay's own 10 x 10 m and a `warp` onto its centre is a
# warp into stone (measured: -0.068 instead of 0.400). **A body at rest reads its surface
# exactly**, so 0.400 is the quay top, and that is the calibration every "on stone" assert in
# this file leans on.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -55 3 78
wait 1.5
mark ACT1 on the east quay at the foot of the tower
assert height > 0.35     # 0.400
assert height < 0.45
assert speed < 0.10
assert gas == 15000

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 2 — THE STAND, 35 m up, at the east deck's own height and 20 m north of the pair.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -68.5 36 85
look 25 -6              # +yaw is -X: this aims WEST, and 25 degrees up the river. See ACT 5.
wait 0.1
mark ACT2 stepping off the east deck
assert height > 35.0     # 35.714
assert height < 36.5
# ⚠️ DERIVED from `gravity_m_s2` -32.0, and it fires BEFORE the hook so nothing but gravity is
# in it: `wait 0.1` is an 8-tick settle, |g| * 8/60 = 4.267 m/s, plus one more simulation step
# |g|/60 = 0.533, i.e. 4.80.
assert speed < 4.8
assert gas == 15000

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 3 — ⭐ THE CROSSING, EAST TO WEST.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
hook right 3.0
wait 0.3
mark ACT3 the rope is on the far tower and he is falling into the arc
assert rope == 1
assert height > 31.0     # 32.690 at 16.028 m/s
assert speed > 15.0

wait 0.3
mark ACT3 half way down
assert height > 25.0     # 26.523 at 26.761
assert speed > 25.0

wait 0.2
mark ACT3 out over the middle of the channel
assert height > 19.0     # 20.731 at 33.461
assert speed > 32.0

# ★ THE FIVE SAMPLES THIS FILE EXISTS FOR, and they are five and not one on purpose: the bottom
# of an arc is exactly the sample a single mark lands beside. The river's surface is at
# **-0.60** and the lowest of these is **14.572 m** — 15.17 m of clear air under the deepest
# point of the crossing, over water 24.9 m wide.
wait 0.1
mark ACT3 over the water, a
assert height > 13.5     # 17.777 at 36.419 m/s
wait 0.1
mark ACT3 over the water, b
assert height > 13.5     # 15.605 at 38.477
wait 0.1
mark ACT3 over the water, c
assert height > 13.5     # **14.572 at 39.459 m/s**  <- the lowest and the fastest tick of the
                         # whole crossing: 6.6x running speed (game.ron: run_speed_m_s 6.0),
                         # and 15.17 m of air between him and the river
wait 0.1
mark ACT3 over the water, d
assert height > 13.5     # 14.865 at 39.265
wait 0.1
mark ACT3 over the water, e
assert height > 13.5     # 16.439 at 37.908 — past the bottom and climbing again

wait 0.7
mark ACT3 the far apex, over the west tower
assert height > 31.0     # 32.666 at 18.112 — the whole fall given back, and he is level with
assert speed < 22.0      # the deck he stepped off
assert gas == 15000      # not one unit spent — the whole crossing is the rope

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 4 — THE FAR BANK.
# ★ **12.000 at exactly 0.000 — he is standing on the far tower's rung 1, whose top is 12.00.**
# ⚠️ And the height alone would not have proved it: the grass either side of the channel dips
# BELOW the water's own surface (-0.60) in places, so "higher than the surface" is not a
# discriminator here and a test built on it would have been measuring the terrain. What proves
# it are the two controls in this file's header — no hook: -1.294; that one deck deleted:
# -1.306. Both drifting, both in the river.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
wait 4.0
mark ACT4 at rest on the far tower's first rung
assert height > 11.9     # 12.000
assert height < 12.1
assert speed < 0.05
assert health == 100     # he crossed it, he did not survive it
assert gas == 15000

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 5 — AND BACK. The mirrored crossing, west to east: the same stand reflected in the
# channel's centre line (x = -72.5) and the same aim negated. So the two acts are an `n = 2`
# and not one measurement quoted twice — if only one bank's towers worked, this act is where
# it shows. It does not: 17.145 m at 37.678 m/s, and he lands on the EAST tower's rung 1.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -76.5 36 85
look -25 -6             # -yaw is +X: the mirror of ACT 2, aimed EAST
wait 0.1
mark ACT5 stepping off the west deck
assert height > 35.0     # 35.714
assert speed < 4.8

hook right 3.0
wait 1.0
mark ACT5 the low point over the water, the other way round
assert height > 13.5     # 17.145 at **37.026 m/s** — the mirror of ACT 3
assert height < 19.0

wait 4.8
mark ACT5 at rest on the east tower's first rung
assert height > 11.9     # 12.000 at 0.000: the other bank's rung, and the river crossed twice
assert height < 12.1
assert speed < 0.05
assert gas == 15000
end
