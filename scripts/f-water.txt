# f-water — THE RIVER. Falls in, slows down, floats, and climbs out with the gear.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f-water.txt --ticks 700
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f-water.txt --ticks 700 >/dev/null 2>&1; echo $?
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --offscreen --script scripts/f-water.txt --ticks 399 \
#       --screenshot docs/images/f003-water.png
#
# PINS: **the four numbers of the Ashgate channel**, out of `maps.ron: maps.ashgate.blocks`,
# and the surface out of `water.ron: volumes.ashgate`. Move any of them and this file is wrong:
#
#   channel floor  (-70.0, -4.1, 0.0)  (10.0, 0.2, 700.0)  -> top    y = -4.00
#   quay west      (-80.0, -1.8, 0.0)  (10.0, 4.4, 700.0)  -> inner  x = -75.00, top y = +0.40
#   quay east      (-60.0, -1.8, 0.0)  (10.0, 4.4, 700.0)  -> inner  x = -65.00, top y = +0.40
#   water surface                                          -> y = -0.60
#   bridges at z = -240 -180 -60 20 110 190 — this run drops at z = 60, 40 m clear of two.
#
# PINS: **`water.ron: swim`** — `drag_per_s` 6.0 decides ACT 2's `speed < 3`, and
# `buoyancy_m_s2` 44.0 against `game.ron: gravity_m_s2` -32.0 decides that he floats at
# `32 * surface_band_m / 44 = 0.727 m` under the surface rather than lying on the bed.
#
# ## What the user asked for, and which act answers it (2026-08-29)
#
#   *„Man schwimmt / wird langsam."*      ACT 2 — the speed, and ACT 3 — the gas
#   *„Nein — Wasser haelt keinen Haken."* NOT here: it is a unit rule
#                                         (`src/vector/hookable.rs`), because water carries no
#                                         collider and therefore no ray can reach it to ask.
#   *„das wasser ist auch VIEL zu klein"* half answered: the water fills the channel it has,
#                                         and the channel is 10 m wide until `maps.ron` says
#                                         otherwise (`docs/FINDINGS.md` FIND-216).
#
# ## ⚠️ ACT 1's `assert speed` is what makes ACT 2 mean anything
#
# Without it, "the body moves at under 3 m/s in the river" is a statement about a body that
# never moved at all. The drop is 20 m over the surface, so the entry speed is
# `sqrt(2 * 32 * 20.6) = 36.3 m/s` and the assert asks for 25.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 1 — 20 m over the channel, dry, and accelerating
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -70 20 60
wait 0.3
mark ACT1 over the channel, dry
assert height > 12
assert gas == 15000

wait 0.75
mark ACT1 the last tick before the water
assert speed > 25
assert height > -0.6

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 2 — in the river. *„wird langsam"*, and it floats instead of drowning.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
wait 2.5
mark ACT2 in the river
assert speed < 3
assert height < -0.5
assert height > -4.1

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 3 — the gear works from inside the river. Two halves, and they are two different
# claims.
#
# 3a  A HOOK FIRED FROM THE WATER STILL BITES. The east quay face is 5 m away and spans
#     y -4.00 .. +0.40; `look -90 0` is +X (`shared::Intent::look_dir`: yaw 0 is -Z), so a
#     level ray from a floating eye at y = 0.27 meets stone. This is the assert that would
#     have caught the `Sensor` version of the water: avian clamps `tmin` to 0 for a ray whose
#     origin is inside a shape, so a collider on the river would answer every escape shot at
#     distance 0 and the player could never leave (`src/shared/water.rs`).
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
look -90 0
hook left 1.0
wait 0.3
mark ACT3a the rope is on, fired from inside the water
assert rope == 1
assert height < -0.5

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3b  AND HE GETS OUT, ON GAS. The rope is let go first (`boost_direction` blends the look
#     direction with the rope's while an arm holds, `game.ron: vector.boost_rope_fraction`,
#     and a blended boost would not be a statement about looking up). Then look up and burn:
#     `boost_m_s2` 46.0 against `gravity_m_s2` -32.0 is +14 m/s² net, less the water's drag.
#
# ⚠️ The gas assert is the point of the act and not decoration. `water.ron:
# swim.gas_cost_factor` is 2.0, so this climb is billed at `gas_boost_per_s * 2` for as long
# as he is under the surface — *„wird langsam"* has a price as well as a speed.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
wait 1.2
look -90 85
key shift 2.0
wait 1.2
mark ACT3b out of the water, on gas
assert height > -0.5
assert gas < 14990

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# EPILOGUE — the frame. No assert in it, on purpose: this act exists so that the run which
# PROVES the river is the same run that SHOWS it, and a picture is not evidence of a rule.
# 22 m over the channel at z = 30, looking north and 30 deg down: the water runs away from
# the camera between its quays, with the bridges at z = 20 and z = -60 across it.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --offscreen --script scripts/f-water.txt --ticks 399 \
#       --screenshot docs/images/f003-water.png
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -70 22 30
look 0 -30
wait 0.2
mark VIEW the channel from above, looking north
end
