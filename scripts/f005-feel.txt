# ⚠️ THE MARK IS ONE TICK BEHIND THE HOOK. `run_script` sits in `IntentSystems::Source`, which
# runs BEFORE `advance_tick`; `vector::hook` logs in `FixedUpdate` with the tick already counted
# up. So a `mark` on the same script tick as a `hook` prints `t = T-1` where the shot leaves at
# `T`. Every ledger below subtracts that one.

# PINS: the STAND HEIGHTS and the anchors (y = 12 in ACT 3/4, the ruin face at y = 28.8). Every
# speed and height bracket below is derived from those; gravity moving re-derives the brackets,
# not the geometry. ACT 4's bracket is additionally pinned to the SWING, not to the wall it ends
# in — see the block above `assert speed > 8.0`.

wait 1.5                # world, camera, meshes and the render pipelines come up (90 ticks)

# ── ACT 1 · the ledger: press -> anchored, on a rope a player really fires ────────────────
look 0 34

wait 0.4
assert speed < 0.5
mark f005-press-held

wait 0.5
mark f005-held-landed

# ── ACT 2 · THE CEILING. The same press, at the far end of `hook_range_m`. ────────────────
# 140 m straight down onto the same roof, from 150 m up. At `hook_speed_m_s` alone that flight
# is 17 ticks and the press is 0.28 s of a game that does nothing; `hook_flight_max_s` makes it

# Measured 2026-08-20: „left the hand: 150.24 m, **6 ticks** to the anchor (t=211)", anchored

# ── ACT 3 · LOOK AT THE ANCHOR AND PULL. „dass man gerader hingezogen wird" ───────────────
# **Airborne**, because that is the only state the mixing rule runs in: on the ground
# `player::locomotion` assigns the velocity outright and the rope steers nothing. The anchor is
# the tall ruin north-west of the square (maps.ron, body 820, its face at y = 28.8) — high
# enough that hauling at it is hauling UP, which is where gravity used to eat the pull.
wait 1.5
warp 76 12 -70
look 0 36
wait 0.1
mark f005-pull-start

#                                 1.5 s of hauling straight at an anchor 21.7 m above him
#   0.7 (this file)           ->  height 26.828 m, i.e. +14.8 m, arriving at the anchor
# The pure-function half of the same claim is the angle:
# tests/player.rs::f005_looking_at_the_anchor_the_pull_is_not_eaten_by_gravity — **26.57° off
# the line he aims at, down to 8.53°**.
wait 1.5
mark f005-pull-end
