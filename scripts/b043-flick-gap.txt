# b043-flick-gap — how far "in der Luft" ONE tick of look staleness (B-041) puts an anchor
# during a play-like flick. Measurement, not a pin: no assert can read an anchor position,
# so the numbers live in the `anchored on body .. at x y z` log lines this run produces.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/b043-flick-gap.txt --ticks 900
#
# Stance: the boulevard (0, ~0.05, -80) — cleared strip, no house between here and the great
# wall. Target: the wall face at z = -100.155 (block_19, y 30..45), 35 m of ray. A yaw sweep
# at 250 deg/s of mouse = 4.17 deg per 60 Hz tick (mouse_deg_per_px 0.08 x ~52 px/tick, an
# ordinary flick, not a violent one) runs -20.85 -> 0 in six steps, one tick apart; the hook
# on the SAME line as the last look fires along the PREVIOUS tick's aim (B-041), so its
# anchor should sit ~4.17 deg to the -x side of where the crosshair points. The control leg
# repeats the sweep and waits 0.3 s: its anchor is the fresh-aim reference. The angular gap
# between the two logged anchors, seen from the eye, is the B-041 contribution; in metres it
# is the lateral offset between the two anchor points on the wall.
settings assist_catch 0
settings assist_strength 0
wait 1.0

# leg 1: flick, hook in the same tick as the last look step. STALE today.
warp 0 0.2 -80
wait 1.0
look -20.85 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -16.68 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -12.51 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -8.34 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -4.17 53.5
wait 0.0167
look 0 53.5
hook right 0.6
wait 0.5
mark b043-flick-stale
assert rope == 1
wait 0.7

# leg 2: same flick, 0.3 s settle — the fresh reference anchor.
warp 0 0.2 -80
wait 1.0
look -20.85 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -16.68 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -12.51 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -8.34 53.5
wait 0.0167
look -4.17 53.5
wait 0.0167
look 0 53.5
wait 0.3
hook left 0.6
wait 0.5
mark b043-flick-fresh
assert rope == 1
wait 0.5
