# f003-map — does the district read as a TOWN, or as a mosaic on a table?
#
#   cargo run -- --headless --script scripts/f003-map.txt --ticks 400
#   cargo run -- --offscreen --screenshot docs/images/f003-map-<before|after>-<mark>.png \
#                --script scripts/f003-map.txt --ticks <the tick of that mark>
#
# ⚠️ Two runs, and they are two runs on purpose: `--screenshot` together with `--script`
# swallows the verdict (`docs/FINDINGS.md` FIND-073), so the exit code comes from the headless
# run and only the picture comes from the offscreen one.
#
# **What it is evidence for.** The user, twice on 2026-08-19: „die map passt aber immernoch
# nicht." Three separate things were measured behind that sentence (`FIND-132`'s last section)
# and this file stands in front of each of them:
#
#   1. the blade that hung across the frame — every shot here is first person, so it is in all
#      of them or in none of them;
#   2. the hand-placed blocks that wear no model — the market square is where they stand
#      thickest next to dressed houses;
#   3. the ground, which is one flat sand plane although `maps.ron: terrain` builds six levels
#      of it. The aerial is chosen so a terrace RISER is side-on and not edge-on.

wait 1.5                # world, camera, meshes and the render pipelines come up

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# MARKET SQUARE, street level — standing on the main street looking EAST down the south row
# of stalls (x = 32 / 40 / 48 at z = -50), with the church tower and the dressed house rows
# behind them. If a hand-placed block is a bare cuboid among modelled roofs, it is bare here.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 26 2 -50    # on the market square, clear of the gantry column at x 10..18
look -90 -3      # yaw -90 = +x, straight down the south row of stalls
wait 0.6
mark f003-map-street
assert height > -1.0   # the market square paving is 0.05 m: he stands ON it
assert height < 2.0

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# THE MARKET QUARTER from 45 m, looking south-west across the square and the terraces behind
# it — NOT from 130 m and NOT across the whole district. `art.ron: fog` is 60..470 m and
# deliberately aggressive (FIND-112, measured 2026-08-19): a vantage that looks 400 m sideways
# is 85 % hazed BY DESIGN, and nothing about the ground can be read out of one. From here the
# far side is ~150 m out, i.e. 26 % into the haze.
#
# The quarter behind the square climbs from level 0 on the main street to level 5
# (`tests/world.rs`, terrain row 11: `1232100123454321`). What that looks like from the air is
# the question FIND-134 answers with a number: 1.50 m of step per 42 m of cell is a 3.6 %
# grade, and 3.6 % under an 11.5 m roofscape is not a slope anybody can see.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 150 45 60
look 48 -17
wait 0.15               # ⚠️ SHORT: he is airborne and every tick is 20 m/s more of falling
mark f003-map-aerial
