# Q-030 — the nape of a SOLID husk is reachable, with air to spare.
#
# cargo run -- --offscreen --script scripts/q030-reach.txt --ticks 168 \
#              --screenshot docs/images/f030-solid-husk.png
#
# It logs `tick 154: cut titan 1 Cortex at 20.67 m/s (player 1)` and the F3 overlay of the
# screenshot reads `pos -11.0 …` and `titan#1 Death`.
#
# ⚠️ The picture is called `f030-solid-husk.png` and not `q030-…`: `tools/norms.py` §10 knows
# f/t/b/p ids and a **question** has no letter of its own. The evidence belongs to `F-030` — it
# is the cut, against the real body instead of against the fixture in `tests/combat.rs`.
#
# `docs/QUESTIONS.md` Q-030 says this run cannot happen: `gear.ron: blades.reach_m` is 1.60 m
# and a husk's body radius plus the player's is `1.25 + 0.35 = 1.60 m`, so "there is zero
# margin". **The arithmetic leaves out two lengths that stand in the same two files.**
#
#   reach_m 1.60  +  titan.ron husk.cortex_radius_m 0.55  +  blades.thickness_m 0.12  =  2.27 m
#   against a clearance of                                    1.25 + 0.35             =  1.60 m
#                                                                            margin  = +0.67 m
#
# The blade never has to touch the titan's axis. It has to touch a **sphere** of radius 0.55 m
# that sits half a head's depth behind the neck, with a swept capsule of radius 0.12 m.
#
# This run flies the pass at **x = −1.80**, i.e. with **0.20 m of air** between the two
# capsules — a margin a player can hold, not the last centimetre of the clearance. The whole
# measured window and the same pass at `large` are in
# `tests/titan.rs::q030_a_flying_player_reaches_the_nape_of_a_solid_husk`.
#
# ## Why the player falls instead of flying
#
# The same reason as `scripts/f030-cortex.txt`: hooking, reeling and boosting cannot be aimed
# from a script accurately enough to pass a 1.10 m sphere, and gravity can. At −20 m/s² a fall
# of 11.2 m is 21 m/s and it is reproducible to the tick. What this run is evidence **of** is
# the geometry — how much air is left beside a solid body — and that does not care which way
# the player is travelling. The horizontal pass at 30 m/s is measured in the test.
#
# ## Why z = 0.55 and x = −1.80
#
# The cortex sits at `(0, 8.9, +0.55)`: `head_m / 2` **behind** the neck
# (`src/titan/rig.rs: cortex_in_head`). Standing at z = 0.55 the blade's line runs straight
# through its centre, so the only thing being measured is the lateral offset. Looking at yaw 0
# (Bevy's forward, −Z) the **right** blade lies on +X (`src/blades/cut.rs: blade_segment`), so
# the player stands on −X and the blade points inward: from x = −1.80 to x = −0.20, and −0.20
# is 0.35 m inside a cortex sphere of radius 0.55.
#
# ## Why the cut lands in `Recover`
#
# Identical to `scripts/f030-cortex.txt`: the husk is 1.88 m away on the ground plane, inside
# `attack_range_m` = 6.0, so he goes `Idle → Pursue → Windup` at once and his torso leans 12°
# over the next 36 ticks — which carries the cortex almost a metre. `Windup` 36 + `Strike` 12
# put `Recover` at tick 141..165 of this run and the crossing at ~154, against an upright body.
#
# 1.5 s of lead-in before anything happens: under `--offscreen` the render pipelines and the
# block meshes are still coming up in the first second.
wait 1.5
spawn titan husk 0 0 0
warp -1.80 18.5 0.55
look 0 0
key F3 0.05
wait 0.90
slash right 0.40
wait 0.08
mark cortex-pass
wait 0.10
assert titans == 1
# **Only now** does the camera turn, and it steps back with it. The blade hangs at the player's
# side, perpendicular to the look direction — you cut what you fly past, not what you look at
# (`src/blades/cut.rs: blade_segment`) — so the look has to stay at yaw 0 until the blade is
# through. 17 m out and level with the nape: from 1.8 m the picture is a grey wall.
warp -11 5.5 8
look -56 -6
wait 1.10
assert titans == 0
