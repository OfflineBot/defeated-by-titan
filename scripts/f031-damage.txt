# F-031 — GESCHWINDIGKEITSABHAENGIGE SCHADENSFORMEL: three body passes floor a husk, and the
# fourth pass takes his nape.
#
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f031-damage.txt --ticks 700
#   ./target/debug/defeated_by_titan --headless --script scripts/f031-damage.txt --ticks 700 >/dev/null 2>&1; echo $?
#
# ## What had to exist before this run could say anything
#
# `gear.ron: blades.damage_per_m_s = 1.4` had **no reader anywhere in `src/`** until 2026-08-25,
# and `titan::rig`'s `Health::full(titan.ron: husk.health = 100)` had **no writer**. A number on
# one side, a pool on the other, and nothing in between — the same shape `docs/FINDINGS.md`
# FIND-075 records for `wear_per_hit`.
#
# The lines to grep for:
#
#   tick N: titan 1 took 28.9 in the Torso at 20.67 m/s (x1.00) — pool 71/100
#   tick N: titan 1 COLLAPSED — 54 ticks on the floor, 240 of refractory
#
# **28.9 is `1.4 x 20.67`**, and 20.67 m/s is the fall this script uses for the same reason
# `scripts/f030-cortex.txt` does: the Vector Gear reaches 30 m/s and more, but hooking and
# boosting cannot be aimed from a script through a 1.10 m sphere. Gravity can — at -20 m/s a
# fall of 11.2 m is 21 m/s, reproducible to the tick.
#
# ## Why the passes start 3 m lower than `f030-cortex.txt`
#
# That file starts at y = 18.5 and its blade meets the **nape** at 8.9 m. Three metres lower is
# the chest: above `leg_m` 4.80 and well under a 0.55 m cortex sphere that `thickness_m` 0.20
# cannot bridge. So these are BODY cuts by construction, and the last act — back at 18.5 — is
# the control that says the same geometry still kills when it is aimed at the nape.
#
# 🔴 **Emptying the pool is not a kill and this run is what shows it.** `assert titans == 1`
# stands after every body pass; the husk is only ever gone after the pass that reaches his
# cortex. If a damage formula ever becomes a second way to kill, those asserts are what fall.
#
# ⚠️ The husk aggros and strikes for 34 of the player's 100 (`titan.ron: husk.damage`), so the
# player may well be down by the last act. That costs nothing here: `warp` works on a downed
# player and the blade still swings.
wait 1.5
spawn titan husk 17.5 0 0
look 0 0
key F3 0.05

# --- pass 1: the chest, at 20.67 m/s -------------------------------------------------
warp 15.75 15.5 0.8
wait 0.90
slash right 0.40
wait 0.30
mark body-pass-1
assert titans == 1

# --- pass 2 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 15.75 15.5 0.8
wait 0.90
slash right 0.40
wait 0.30
mark body-pass-2
assert titans == 1

# --- pass 3: the pool should be empty around here, and he goes on the floor -----------
warp 15.75 15.5 0.8
wait 0.90
slash right 0.40
wait 0.30
mark body-pass-3
assert titans == 1

# --- pass 4: same geometry, aimed at the NAPE. The cortex is the only thing that kills.
warp 15.75 18.5 0.8
wait 0.90
slash right 0.40
wait 0.20
mark cortex-cut
wait 1.20
assert titans == 0
