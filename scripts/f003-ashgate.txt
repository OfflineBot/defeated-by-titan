# f003-ashgate — the district, measured: a 120 m wall, a river you fall into, a lane that
# swings, and a street that is finally narrower than its houses are tall.
#
# `assets/data/maps.ron: current` is `"ashgate"` since 2026-08-12, so this runs as committed.
#
#   cargo run -- --headless --script scripts/f003-ashgate.txt --ticks 1800
#   cargo run -- --offscreen --screenshot docs/images/f003-ashgate.png \
#                --script scripts/f003-ashgate.txt --ticks 1720
#
# `--screenshot` together with `--script` swallows the verdict, so the two runs are two runs:
# the first one is the exit code, the second one is the picture.
#
# ## What it is evidence FOR
#
# `assets/data/missions.ron` has named `map: "ashgate"` since the first mission existed and
# the map did not exist. This is the map, and these are the five claims it has to survive:
#
#   1. it is really THIS map that got built (nothing stands at (200, ., -120) in the graybox),
#   2. the wall is 120 m tall, 45 m thick and has a hole in it where the gate is,
#   3. the river is a lowered channel you fall into and the bridges are not,
#   4. **the lane swings** — `docs/FINDINGS.md` FIND-041: `arc bottom = H - L`, and a swing
#      exists only while the horizontal gap to the anchor is smaller than the anchor height.
#      The gantry beams stand 60 m up at a 35 m pitch, so `60 - 35 = 25` m of arc bottom.
#   5. **the quarter is a town and not a business park** — ACT 5 hooks one house from the
#      roof of the house opposite across a **7.00 m** street, and both the arc and the way it
#      ends are the answer to whether the gantries can be deleted (they cannot; FIND-060).
#
# ## The baselines the numbers are read against
#
#   running                          6.000 m/s   (game.ron: player.run_speed_m_s)
#   the graybox lane (FIND-041)     31.4  m/s    on the first arc, 43.3 m/s on leg 4
#   the church arc (FIND-026)       24.85 m/s    — the best the old city could do, once

wait 1.5                # world, camera, meshes and the render pipelines come up

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 0 — the map that got built is Ashgate, and its wall is 120 m tall.
# Dropped from 130 m over the wall crown at x = 200, where the graybox has nothing at all.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 200 130 -120
wait 1.4
mark f003-wall-crown
assert height > 118     # the coping is the top: 118..120 m
assert height < 126
assert speed < 1.0      # standing, not still falling

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 1 — the inner gate is a hole through 45 m of wall, not a texture.
# Dropped 5 m inside the wall footprint on the gate axis: without the opening he would be
# pushed out on top of the wall, 120 m up.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 0 5 -120
wait 1.2
mark f003-gate-passage
assert height < 1.5

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 2 — the river. There is no water in this game, so it is a lowered channel: 4 m down,
# not anchorable. The bridge over it is at street level and IS anchorable.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp -70 6 60
wait 1.5
mark f003-channel
assert height < -3.0    # the channel floor is at -4.0
warp -70 6 110
wait 1.5
mark f003-bridge
assert height > -0.6    # the bridge deck: top edge 0.4
assert height < 1.5

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 3 — THE SWING. Gantry station z = 17.5, hooked level from 30 m away.
# H = 58 m of anchor, L = 26 m of rope, so the arc bottom is 32 m ABOVE the ground and
# 20 m above the roofs. Rope only: no Shift, no Ctrl, `gas == 300` at both ends.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 0 58 47.5
look 0 0                # yaw 0 = -Z, down the main street toward the wall
wait 0.1
mark f003-arc-start
assert height > 57.0
assert speed < 3.0
assert gas == 300

hook right 4.0
wait 1.95
mark f003-arc-bottom
assert height > 33.0    # 33.328 m — the number FIND-041 says a city needs and the graybox
assert height < 33.7    # lacked. 21.8 m of air over the tallest residential roof (11.5 m)
assert speed > 31.0     # 31.375 m/s = 5.2x running, and the same arc the graybox lane gives
assert speed < 31.7     # (FIND-041: 31.4 m/s) — same pitch, same beam height, same physics
assert rope == 1

wait 1.85
mark f003-arc-apex
assert height > 57.0    # 57.550 m: the far side gives the height back to within 0.5 m
assert gas == 300       # not one unit spent

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 4 — THE WALL IN TWO MOVES. Ground -> the 60 m gallery -> the 120 m crown corbel.
# Both legs are far inside the 200 m range; what decides it is the line of sight past the
# battered face, and that is what the corbel (it projects 6 m past the face) is for.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ⚠️ He stands on the market square at x = 75 and NOT at x = 40: the church tower occupies
# x 38..52, and a first run put him inside it — a hook fired from inside a body finds
# nothing, and the whole act read as "the gallery is not hookable".
warp 75 2 -30
look 0 44               # the gallery edge is 57.6 m away and 56 m up: atan = 44.2 degrees
wait 1.2                # ⚠️ long enough for ACT 3's arm to finish RETRACTING: a trigger
                        # pulled on an arm that is not Idle is dropped without a word, and
                        # a first run lost the whole act to exactly that
mark f003-ground
assert height < 1.5

hook left 4.0           # move 1: the gallery at 60 m — the OTHER arm, see above
wait 0.9                # ⚠️ the hook is a projectile at 160 m/s — 80 m of rope is 0.5 s of
mark f003-gallery-hooked  # flight, and a 0.5 s wait measured `rope == 0` on a hook that hit
assert rope == 1
key Ctrl 3.0
wait 3.4                # longer than the 4.0 s hold, so the rope is gone before move 2
mark f003-platform
assert height > 58.0    # 63.090 m — the 60 m platform, out of the street in ONE move
assert height < 68.0

look 0 82               # ⚠️ SWEPT, and the arithmetic behind it is the whole reason the
                        # corbel exists. Standing on the gallery at y = 60 the eye is 58.4 m
                        # under the crown; the corbel underside spans z -108..-96, and the
                        # battered face stands at z = -105.5 at y = 118. So the ray has to be
                        # steeper than 74.6 degrees (or it goes into the face) and shallower
                        # than 84 (or it passes the corbel's inner edge). 82 hits the
                        # underside at z = -98.2, measured, from anywhere on the gallery.
hook right 5.0          # move 2: the crown corbel at 120 m
wait 0.9
mark f003-crown-hooked
assert rope == 1
key Ctrl 3.0
wait 3.4
mark f003-crown
assert height > 110.0   # 117.001 m — the crown, in the SECOND move
assert gas > 250.0      # 263.798: the whole climb costs 36.2 of 300, both reels together
assert gas < 280.0

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# ACT 5 — THE STREET. One block's east wing (x 114.5..125.5, ridge 10.711) faces the next
# block's west wing (x 132.5..143.5, ridge 11.303) across **7.00 m**, and that gap is the
# whole point of the closed-block layout: the survey of the model town gives street : ridge
# = 0.62 : 1, this map measures **7.00 m / 0.82 : 1** against the shorter of two ridges
# (`tests/world.rs::f003_the_street_is_narrower_than_the_houses_are_tall`).
#
# He steps off the west roof over the street and hooks the roof opposite. What this act
# measures is BOTH halves of the answer, and the second one is why the gantries stay:
#
#   * there IS an arc, and its bottom is **5.92 m over the pavement** on **zero gas** —
#     `H - L = 11.303 - 10.76`, and the peak is 14.278 m/s = 2.4x running speed. `d < H`
#     holds between two houses, exactly as FIND-058 predicted.
#   * and it ends **against the facade of the house he hooked**, 14.278 -> 0.032 m/s in one
#     step, because the bottom of a pendulum lies vertically under its anchor and that anchor
#     stands over solid house (FIND-042, and FIND-029 measured the same thing on the church).
#
# So a rope between two houses is a 7 m hop, not a lane. The lane needs an anchor over open
# ground, and that is what the gantry beams are.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
wait 1.5                 # ⚠️ BEFORE the warp, not after: ACT 4's arm has to finish
                         # retracting, and 1.2 s of waiting in mid-air over a 7 m street is
                         # 1.2 s of falling — a first run measured `height -0.000` and read
                         # as "the street does not swing" when what it measured was gravity.
warp 127 13 107.5
look 90 -20              # yaw +90 = +X, across the street; -20 clears the near eaves and
wait 0.05                # lands on the roof opposite, 10.76 m of rope away.
mark f003-street-start
assert height > 12.5     # 13.000 — airborne over the street, between two roofs
assert speed < 2.0       # 1.333 — three ticks of falling, no more

hook right 6.0
wait 0.35
mark f003-street-hooked
assert rope == 1         # rope 10.76 m to an 11.303 m ridge
assert height > 10.5     # 11.119
assert height < 11.6
assert speed > 7.0       # 8.667 — already falling into the canyon

wait 0.25
mark f003-street-arc
assert height > 7.0      # 7.897 m — over the pavement, and 4.5 m below the roofs
assert speed > 13.0      # 14.278 m/s = 2.4x running, out of a 7 m street, on the rope alone
assert speed < 15.5

wait 0.25
mark f003-street-wall
assert height > 5.5      # 5.915 — the arc bottom, and it is above the street
assert height < 6.5
assert speed < 1.0       # 0.032: the swing ended against the facade he was hanging from
assert gas > 260         # 263.798 — unchanged since ACT 4: the whole street act runs on the
assert gas < 268         # rope alone, no Shift and no Ctrl

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# The vantage point for `--screenshot`: over the quarter east of the main street, looking
# southwest across the roofscape at the wall. The old vantage looked down the main street
# and photographed the gantries; what has to be visible now is the closed blocks.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
warp 200 150 250         # north-east corner of the district, 150 m up, looking southwest
look -25 -30             # over the quarters at the wall. yaw 0 = -Z, +90 = +X.
wait 0.15                # ⚠️ SHORT: he is airborne here, and every tick is 20 m/s more of
mark f003-vantage        # falling. **The picture is taken at this mark** — the `--ticks` of
wait 1.5                 # the screenshot run has to land on it, not on the end of the
                         # script. A first run photographed ACT 5's facade from 5.9 m up.
